Featured Experience

Designing Fiber Optic Networks Using Google Earth Pro

Designed a fiber optic network using Google Earth Pro to support the planning and visualization of FTTH (Fiber to the Home) infrastructure.

The resulting design provides a visualization of the FTTH network topology, illustrating the connections between distribution equipment and customer locations.

The project involved mapping potential fiber routes, identifying locations of ODP, ODC, and customer premises, and estimating cable distances based on geographic conditions. This approach helped create a more accurate and efficient network design while providing a clear visual representation of the planned fiber optic infrastructure.

Furthermore, the use of Google Earth Pro aids in analyzing geographical conditions and determining more effective network routes. Such visualization makes the network planning process easier to understand and helps minimize potential errors when determining installation paths in the field.
